\begin{center}
{\Large\bfseries Do Circuits Survive Compression? A Null-Model Audit of Circuit Stability Under Quantization and Pruning}\\[2pt]
{\normalsize Supratik Bhowal --- AAAI Undergraduate Consortium}\\[2pt]
{\small Progress Report: Methodology, Results, Future Scope}\\[2pt]
{\small \today}
\end{center}
\begin{abstract}\noindent Interpretability has measured representations under compression but not computation itself, and the one prior circuit claim is qualitative with no noise floor. We built a null-first Algorithm-1 pipeline with Circuit Stability Index (CSI), two-level comparison, and freeze-as-preregistration, validated end-to-end on synthetic tasks with 459 tests passed and 1 skipped and frozen null still false. Reproduced baselines show dense perplexity 8.21 for Gemma-2-2B and 9.28 for Llama-3.2-1B, Wanda over magnitude 0.794 vs.\ 0.210 at 30\% sparsity, and fixed-dictionary Jaccard correlation $-0.975$ with output damage; synthetic check gives CSI 0.333 [0.25,0.40]. No causal confirmation yet: CSI $\approx 1$ will mean noise, CSI $\gg 1$ selective damage, CSI $< 1$ gentler than noise.
\end{abstract}
\section{Introduction}
Threshold choice changes the circuit by $\sim$40$\times$ in size and dense vs.\ 75\%-sparse graphs agree at routing level 0.666 vs.\ chance 0.199 but fall to chance at exact-edge level 0.163 vs.\ 0.126 \cite{parekh2026circus,sheng2026circuit}. Patching mixes direct and interaction effects as NIE$=$PIE$+$INT \cite{vaidyanathan2026curse}. We audit computation, not features, on Gemma-2-2B, Llama-3.2-1B and Pythia with IOI, greater-than and docstring tasks, testing $\displaystyle s(e)=\#\{\text{graphs containing }e\}/|B||S|$, $D(c)=\mathrm{Dist}(f_{\mathrm{dense}},f_{c})$, $\mathrm{CSI}(c,T)=D(c)/\mathrm{median}(D_{\mathrm{null}}(c))$.
\section{Methodology}
\begin{description}[style=nextline,leftmargin=0pt,labelindent=0pt,labelwidth=0pt,labelsep=0pt,itemsep=4pt,parsep=0pt,topsep=3pt]
\item[Null-first pipeline.] Stages run in fixed order with hash guards.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Reference} --- Stage A builds dense $s(e)$ over $B$ configs $\times$ $S$ seeds.
\item \textbf{Freeze} --- Stage B builds $D_{\mathrm{null}}$ from $R$ nulls then freezes irreversibly; Stage C is blocked without frozen hash.
\item \textbf{Measure} --- Stage C computes $D(c)$ and CSI at both levels; Stage D runs causal patching and cross-audit.
\end{enumerate}
\item[Three nulls and ensemble.] Controls separate damage from noise.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Magnitude} --- random $\Delta W$ matched per-tensor Frobenius norm to compression $\Delta W$.
\item \textbf{Perplexity} --- alternate family tuned to same perplexity within 0.05.
\item \textbf{Ensemble} --- $B{=}16$, $S{=}5$, $R{=}20$ pilot (provisional $B{=}4$, $S{=}2$, $R{=}3$) over non-nested threshold grid.
\end{enumerate}
\item[Extraction and stability.] Two pipelines plus dictionary-free variant.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Graphs} --- attribution-patching graph, edge-pruning graph, and dense-node variant; thresholds node 0.8 and edge 0.98.
\item \textbf{Frequencies} --- report $s(e)\in[0,1]$ vector with hypotheses
\begin{enumerate}[label=(H\arabic*)]
\item \textbf{Selective} --- CSI $\gg 1$ with bootstrap CI over $B,S,r$ excludes 1.
\item \textbf{Noise} --- CSI $\approx 1$ indistinguishable from null.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
\item[Distances, levels, and audits.] All comparisons carry uncertainty.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Distance} --- primary L1 over union, ablation Jensen-Shannon, plus normalized L1; CSI with 1000 bootstraps.
\item \textbf{Levels} --- exact-edge overlap and routing-head overlap with strict guard on empty projection.
\item \textbf{Audit} --- Spearman cross-audit, threshold sweep at 0.0--0.9, chance floor, Benjamini-Hochberg at $q{=}0.05$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{description}
\section{Results}
\textbf{Reproduction.} Corrected dense perplexity is 8.21 for Gemma-2-2B, 9.99 for Gemma-3-1B and 9.28 for Llama-3.2-1B; magnitude 50\% drives Gemma-2-2B to 377.03 and Llama-3.2-1B to 2412.44 while Wanda 30\% stays at 8.82 and 10.65; downstream accuracy falls from 0.759 dense to 0.325 at magnitude 50\% and 0.627 at Wanda 50\% (Table~1); \textbf{Wanda 30\% keeps MNN 0.794 vs.\ magnitude 0.210 and Jaccard 0.591 vs.\ 0.317}; seed MNN floor is 0.0273 and Kendall $W$ is 0.9416.

\textbf{Divergence (novelty).} Novel code is CSI with bootstrap, matched-magnitude and matched-perplexity harnesses, $s(e)$ bands, routing level, chance floor, interaction flag $|$INT$|>0.5\max$, and freeze-as-preregistration over 50 science nodes, 33 experiment nodes and 28 compression nodes in 22 communities; prior level disagreement is reproduced as exact-edge 0.163/0.142 vs.\ chance 0.126/0.117 at $p{=}0.040/0.106$ and routing 0.666/0.554 vs.\ 0.199/0.185 at $p<0.001$ with cross-seed Jaccard 0.363 (Table~1 and Table~2).

\textbf{SPR.} Synthetic plumbing run $B{=}4$, $S{=}2$, $R{=}3$ gives exact CSI 0.333 [0.25,0.40] with $D{=}0.25$ and null median 0.75 and routing CSI 0.0 [0,0] with $D{=}0.0$; chance exact Jaccard is 0.714 vs.\ floor 0.0 [0,0.091] at $p{=}0.002$ and routing 1.0 vs.\ 0.0 at $p{=}0.002$; calibration shows fixed-dictionary Jaccard $\rho{=}-0.975$ [-1.00,-0.873], MNN $-0.684$ [-0.888,-0.276] and fragility $-0.032$ [-0.473,0.514] (Table~2); no real CSI is measured yet and cross-audit stays pending.

\section{Future Scope}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Pins and prompts} (data, 4 h) --- record HF revisions, 200--500 prompts per task, and WikiText-2 window 1024 stride 512 protocol. \\ Why: weight re-upload or template sampling shift voids dense reference. \\ Deliverable: task configs marked confirmed plus passing protocol test.
\item \textbf{Preregistration fix} (methods, 0.5 h) --- set L1 plus JS as final and resample bootstrap over $B,S,r$. \\ Why: seed-only intervals are too narrow for ensemble variance. \\ Deliverable: 14 distance tests green before freeze.
\item \textbf{Consensus bands} (methods, 0.5 h) --- set core $s{=}1.0$ and noise $s{=}7/16$ at $B{=}16$ on crossed grid. \\ Why: matches CIRCUS taxonomy and checks Jaccard $\sim$0.33. \\ Deliverable: decompose and grid tests green.
\item \textbf{Seven-cell run} (compute, 2 weeks) --- RTN INT8/INT4, GPTQ, AWQ, magnitude and Wanda with frozen null. \\ Why: tests selective vs.\ noise vs.\ gentler predictions head to head. \\ Deliverable: CSI table with CIs at both levels.
\item \textbf{Causal audit and paper} (analysis, 2 weeks) --- grouped patching with INT split, cross-audit, sweep, and AAAI-UC draft. \\ Why: links movers to behavior and ships reusable harness. \\ Deliverable: camera-ready draft plus one-command rebuild.
\end{enumerate}
